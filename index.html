Dishonored 2: Minecraft Mashup

A stealth assassin fantasy where combat can be enhanced with summon spells and golden apples, without changing the structure of the original levels.

See the pitch GitHub Repo

Core idea

The game keeps Dishonored 2 intact.

This concept keeps the original Dishonored 2 missions exactly as they are. No level redesigns, no new layouts, no altered story structure. Instead, the player gains three summon spells and finds golden apples while exploring the same world.

The spells are always available. The golden apples are randomly found and give a temporary +50% health buffer that disappears once the extra health is lost. The player still plays the same stealth-focused assassin experience, but with a weird, chaotic summon system layered on top.

Spell roster

Three summon spells

Slime Spell

  • Level 1: 3 small slimes
  • Level 2: 2 medium slimes
  • Level 3: 1 large slime

Best for crowd control, chaos, and blocking routes.

Zombie Spell

  • Level 1: 3 basic zombies
  • Level 2: 3 zombies with swords
  • Level 3: 3 zombies with leather armor and swords

Frontline bruisers that absorb damage and pressure enemies.

Skeleton Spell

  • Level 1: 2 skeletons with basic bows
  • Level 2: 2 skeletons with Power I bows
  • Level 3: 2 skeletons with Power I bows and flame arrows

Ranged support for damage, kiting, and finishing threats.

Survival mechanic

Golden Apple

🍏

A golden apple gives the player a temporary +50% max health boost. It is not a full heal, and it does not stack. When the extra health is lost, the apple disappears, leaving the player back at normal health.

It is a panic-button survival tool. Players can choose to save it for a desperate escape, or burn it immediately to push through a risky fight.

Mission design

Original levels stay untouched

  • Same mission structure
  • Same stealth routes and combat spaces
  • Same overarching Dishonored 2 experience
  • New summon spells and apples layered in as optional tools
  • The player decides when to use them
